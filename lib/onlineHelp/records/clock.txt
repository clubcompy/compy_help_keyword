        About the Clock Record
        \u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E
The Clock record captures information
about the current time and date of the
VCE's computer

Record<$Clock> definition:
 - Signals when: approximately every 10
   milliseconds

   field  \179        description
 \196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\197\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196\196
    time  \179 <number> current epoch time,
          \179 milliseconds elapsed since
          \179 Jan 1, 1970 UTC
    year  \179 <number> year of current
          \179 date in Local Time
   month  \179 <number> month code of
          \179 current date 0 \029 11 in Local
          \179 Time where 0 = January and
          \179 11 = December
    dofm  \179 <number> day of the month of
          \179 current date 1 \029 31 in Local
          \179 Time
    dofw  \179 <number> day of the week
          \179 code of the current date
          \179 0 \029 6 in Local Time where
          \179 0 = Sunday and 6 = Saturday
     h    \179 <number> hour of the day for
          \179 the current date in Local
          \179 Time
     m    \179 <number> minutes for the
          \179 current date in Local Time
     s    \179 <number> seconds for the
          \179 current date in Local Time
   tzoff  \179 <number> the difference in
          \179 minutes between the current
          \179 date in UTC and the current
          \179 date in Local Time
          \179
          \179 Positive numbers represent
          \179 time zones west of the
          \179 United Kingdom and east of
          \179 the International Date Line
          \179 set in the pacific ocean.
          \179 Negative numbers represent
          \179 time zones east of the
          \179 United Kingdom and west of
          \179 the International Date line

Notes:
 - UTC = Coordinated Universal Time
   (supercedes GMT, Greenwich Mean Time)
 - Local Time = The VCE computer's time
   and date set in the time zone of
   where the computer is located
