        About the Joystick Array
        \u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E\u203E
In addition to mouse and keyboard input
device support, your VCE supports
reading from optional joysticks and
gamepads attached to your physical
device

The Joystick built-in array contains the
list of record<$Joystick>'s that have
been registered with your physical
computer. See notes below about how to
register joysticks and gamepads on your
device to fill up your Joystick array

Record<$Joystick> definition:
 - Signals when: an axis or button state
   changes on the joystick, or a
   joystick is connected or disconnected
   from your physical device

Notes:
 - How to register a joystick or gamepad
   with the VCE:

  1) Connect your joystick or gamepad to
     your device (either by wired cable
     or wireless pairing and connection)
  2) Press any button on your joystick
     or gamepad. This will cause a
     record<$Joystick> to be added to
     the Joystick array that you can
     read from and LISTEN to in your
     Comfy programs

 - Make sure to instruct the users of
   your Comfy programs to press a button
   on their unregistered joysticks to
   get them to appear in the Joystick
   array

 - The evt field on record<$Joystick>
   indicates what interesting event has
   occurred and what data has recently
   changed on a joystick. Use the LISTEN
   keyword to be notified of every evt
   that occurs

   Here is a description of the possible
   evt values and their meanings:
