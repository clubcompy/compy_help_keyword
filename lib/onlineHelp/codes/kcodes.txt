On the built-in Keyboard record, there
is the kcode field. This field
represents a unique numeric code for
each key pressed on the keyboard

When writing interactive programs like
video games, doing a LISTEN Keyboard is
needed to react immediately when your
users press and release keys. You can
use that information to make something
happen in your game
